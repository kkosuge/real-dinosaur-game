# Real Dinosaur Game — Build Spec (shared contract)

A photorealistic remake of Chrome's offline "T-Rex Runner" (Dinosaur Game) that runs in the browser.
Visual target: `reference/screen.webp` (1994×789). Bright, hazy, desaturated desert at midday; sun glare in the
upper-left; pale sky with a few small cumulus clouds; low distant mesa range on the horizon; flat pale sand/gravel
ground with scattered pebbles and dry grass tufts; a realistic grey-brown T-rex running right with a small dust puff at its feet;
a tall saguaro cactus ahead; score "HI 00043 00029" top-right in a dark-grey bold monospace font.

Gameplay target: faithful to Chrome's T-Rex Runner (jump / variable-height jump / duck / fast-drop, cacti in
groups of 1–3, pterodactyls at 3 altitudes after a speed threshold, speed ramp, distance score, hi-score,
achievement blink + sound every 100, day→night cycle every 700 points, GAME OVER + restart).

## 2. World coordinates

- Logical world height **H = 540** units (the playfield). Playfield width
  **W = min(round(H·3.2), max(round(H·vw/vh), min(1350, max(810, floor(104·vw/44)))))** (vw × vh = viewport CSS px):
  never less than Chrome's view ahead of the dino (≈1350u ≈ 506 Chrome px) on desktop, and on narrow phones only as
  short as needed to keep the dino (104u) at least ≈44 CSS px tall. Reason: reaction time must not depend on the
  window shape.
- When the viewport is taller than W/H at that width, the scene extends to fill it: extra sky above
  (45 % of the extra height) and extended ground below (55 %), so the canvas always fills the viewport. GAME OVER and
  hints stay attached to the playfield; so does the HUD, except when the extra sky is taller than 60u: then its line pins
  to HUD_TOP below the top of the screen (plus `env(safe-area-inset-top)`), not floating mid-sky. Only viewports wider
  than 3.2:1 are pillarboxed; their side bands split sky / ground colour at the canvas horizon (CSS `--hz`, set by
  main.js; `theme-color` follows the sky colour).
  Horizontal safe-area insets (landscape notch) are padding on `#stage`, and the canvas fits inside them.
  Backing store = canvas CSS size × devicePixelRatio (capped for memory; touch screens at most 2×).
- y grows downward. Reference proportions (fractions of H):
  - `HORIZON_Y  = 0.740 H` — where the distant plain meets the mountains / sky.
  - `GROUND_Y   = 0.760 H` — the running line: dino feet and obstacle bases touch this line.
  - Mountains band ≈ 0.64H–0.74H. Clouds 0.20H–0.50H. Sun glare at top-left corner.
- Dino: standing/running visible height ≈ **0.19 H (~104u)**, body length (tail tip → snout) ≈ 1.75× its height.
  The dino's tail tip sits at x ≈ 40u; it never moves horizontally during play (world scrolls).
- Cactus: large ≈ 0.21–0.25 H tall, small ≈ 0.13–0.15 H tall (engine tunes for fairness, see §5).
- Units per frame are expressed at 60 fps; the loop uses a fixed timestep (1/60 s) with an accumulator
  (clamp large dt, e.g. after tab switch) so physics is frame-rate independent and deterministic.

## 3. Visual layers (back → front) and how assets map to them

1. **Sky** (static, cover-fit, bottom anchored so its `horizonY` lands on HORIZON_Y): `sky.day`, `sky.dusk`, `sky.night`
   crossfaded by the day/night cycle. Day sky contains the sun glare upper-left. Night: stars (image) + twinkle.
   Dusk → night: the dusk sky's sun is covered by its glow colour as it sets (no ghost disc), and the stars come out
   late, never over the sunset glow.
2. **Moon** (night only, upper-right-ish, slow drift) — `sky.moon`.
3. **Clouds** — `sky.clouds[]` sprites at several depths/scales, drift left at 0.05–0.2× game speed + tiny wind.
4. **Mountains** — `terrain.mountains`, horizontally seamless strip, scrolls at ~0.03× speed, its `baselinePx`
   aligned to HORIZON_Y. Atmospheric haze.
5. **Ground** — `terrain.ground`, horizontally seamless, row 0 = horizon, last row = nearest to camera. Drawn from
   HORIZON_Y to H in horizontal slices with a per-row scroll factor (perspective parallax): factor ≈ 0.35 at the
   horizon, exactly 1.0 at GROUND_Y (so obstacles look glued to the ground), rising to ≈ 2.5–3 at the bottom edge.
   The ground texture must be fine-grained (no big distinct objects) so slice shearing is invisible. Rows below the
   running line are drawn horizontally magnified by their parallax factor (perspective), so the fine texture never
   shears; the texture bakes a depth-of-field ramp (sharp at the running line, soft toward the camera).
6. **Background decor** — `terrain.decor[]` rocks / dry grass tufts placed at depths between HORIZON_Y and GROUND_Y,
   scaled down and scrolled with the matching row factor. A band of small dark pebbles straddles the running line.
7. **Shadows, obstacles, dino** at GROUND_Y. Soft elliptical contact shadows (shadow shrinks/fades while jumping),
   plus long thin cast shadows falling to the right of the dino and each cactus (none for pterodactyls).
8. **Particles** — dust while running (each toe-off kicks up a pale plume with dark grain spray behind the rear foot),
   a burst on landing, crash dust.
9. **Foreground decor** — decor between GROUND_Y and H, larger and faster (factor > 1), using the pre-blurred
   `srcBlur` variant (depth-of-field look like the reference's blurry foreground rocks); entries with `fg: false`
   (e.g. wispy tufts) are never used here.
10. **Grading** — day/dusk/night color grade applied over layers 4–9 (e.g. multiply + a soft blue/orange tint),
    subtle vignette. Night must still be clearly playable: the actors (dino, obstacles, dust) share the terrain's
    night hue with a soft moon-side rim light; clouds are moonlit, dim, and cover the stars behind them.
11. **HUD** — "HI 00043 00029" top-right, bold monospace, dark grey (#535353-ish, matches reference), light at night.
    As in Chrome, no current score before the first run, and HI only when it is > 0. Blinks on each 100 milestone;
    the blink is cancelled on GAME OVER, which always shows the real score (`Sim.hudScore(sim, out)`). GAME OVER text
    + restart icon centered. Start hint text on the idle screen.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: no camera shake, no hint pulse, no star twinkle.

## 4. Asset manifest contract (fragments in `assets/manifest/<group>.json`)

All paths are relative to the project root (e.g. `"assets/dino/run_0.webp"`). All pixel coordinates are in the
image's own pixels. `hitboxes` are arrays of `[x, y, w, h]` rectangles (image pixels) that tightly but slightly
generously *under*-cover the opaque body (thin tail tip, fingers, spines, wing membranes' ragged edges excluded) —
typically 3–6 rects per frame, computed by the pipeline from the alpha mask (e.g. split into horizontal bands and
take trimmed min/max x of alpha>128 per band, then shrink ~4%). Every sprite is RGBA with clean, halo-free,
antialiased edges (no magenta/green fringes, no leftover background).

### dino.json
```json
{ "dino": {
  "frameW": 768, "frameH": 448,          // EVERY dino frame is exported on this identical canvas size
  "baselinePx": 440,                     // ground-contact y (feet) — identical for all frames on the ground
  "standHeightPx": 300,                  // height (feet→top of head) of the running/standing dino in px
  "frames": {
    "idle": [ {"src": "...", "hitboxes": [[x,y,w,h], ...]} ],          // 1–2 frames, standing still
    "run":  [ {...}, {...}, {...}, {...} ],                              // 4–6 frame looping run cycle
    "duck": [ {...}, {...} ],                                            // 2 frames, low crouched run
    "jump": [ {...} ],                                                   // airborne pose
    "dead": [ {...} ]                                                    // crash pose
  }
}}
```
All dino frames share one pixel scale (the body is the same size in every frame) and are horizontally aligned so
the torso/hips stay at the same x across the run cycle (no jitter). Faces RIGHT. Scale in the engine:
`s = DINO_HEIGHT / standHeightPx`, frame drawn at `frameW*s × frameH*s` with `baselinePx*s` on the dino's y.

### cactus.json
```json
{ "cactus": {
  "large": [ {"src": "...", "w": 260, "h": 520, "hitboxes": [...]} , ...],   // ≥3 variants (tall saguaros)
  "small": [ {"src": "...", "w": 150, "h": 300, "hitboxes": [...]} , ...]    // ≥3 variants (short/young cacti)
}}
```
Trimmed tightly to the plant; the base of the plant touches the bottom row (h). Engine draws each at its class
height (optionally × per-sprite `"scale"`), groups 1–3 side by side with slight overlap/jitter.

### ptero.json
```json
{ "ptero": {
  "frameW": 640, "frameH": 400, "wingspanPx": 600, "bodyCenterYPx": 200,
  "frames": [ {"src": "...", "hitboxes": [...]}, ... ]   // 2–4 wing-flap frames, identical canvas, body aligned
}}
```
A realistic Pteranodon (not a bird), flying LEFT (toward the dino). Engine sets wingspan ≈ 0.8× dino length.

Optional, additive keys (the engine uses them when present and falls back otherwise):
- dino: `"tailTipPx"` — leftmost opaque x of the run frames (pins the tail tip to x ≈ 40u and measures the dino's length).
  Dead frame: `"opaqueX0"` — its leftmost visible x (alpha > 24); the renderer never pulls the crash pose back so far
  that the tail leaves the canvas (visual only).
- cactus entries: `"scale"` (per-sprite height multiplier), `"baseX"` / `"baseW"` (trunk-base centre / width, contact-shadow anchor).
- ptero frames: `"opaqueBox": [x, y, w, h]` — visible extent (alpha > 24). Altitudes keep the visible wingtips clear of the
  ground (low), a ducking dino's back (mid) and a standing dino's head (high), not just the hitboxes.

`tools/build_manifest.py` writes every existing `src` / `srcBlur` into `assets/manifest.js` as `path?v=<sha1[:10]>`
(content hash; the fragments keep bare paths). The service worker caches images cache-first by that URL, so a changed
image is a new URL and is never served stale; `manifest.js` and code are network-first, revalidated with
`cache: 'no-cache'` so an update never mixes new and heuristically cached old files. `--check` also flags image
bytes changed without a rebuild.

### sky.json
```json
{ "sky": {
  "day":   {"src": "...jpg|webp", "w": 2560, "h": 1080, "horizonY": 0.80},   // opaque; horizonY = normalized y of horizon
  "dusk":  {"src": "...", "w": ..., "h": ..., "horizonY": ...},
  "night": {"src": "...", "w": ..., "h": ..., "horizonY": ...},             // starry night sky, no moon
  "moon":  {"src": "...png|webp", "w": 256, "h": 256},
  "clouds": [ {"src": "...", "w": 900, "h": 300}, ... ]                   // ≥4 varied cumulus, transparent
}}
```
Skies contain ONLY sky (no mountains, no ground, no clouds baked in except very faint haze); they extend a little
below the horizon (to be covered by mountains/ground).
Optional per sky entry (`day` / `dusk` / `night`): `"sun": {"x": 0.3, "y": 0.6, "r": 0.05, "glow": [r, g, b]}` — the
sun's position and radius as fractions of the image, plus its glow colour. Absent or malformed (or while a placeholder
replaces that image): the renderer uses a built-in constant.

### terrain.json
```json
{ "terrain": {
  "mountains": {"src": "...", "w": 4096, "h": 512, "baselinePx": 500},    // RGBA, seamless L↔R, transparent sky
  "ground":    {"src": "...", "w": 4096, "h": 560},                       // opaque, seamless L↔R, row0=horizon
  "decor": [ {"src": "...", "srcBlur": "...", "w": 200, "h": 120, "kind": "rock"}, ... ]  // ≥4 rocks, ≥4 grass
}}
```
Optional per decor entry: `"fg": false` — the sprite is never used for the large foreground layer (e.g. wispy grass
tufts). Absent means allowed.

### Asset sizing / format
- Sprites: export so the on-screen size at 1440p fits without upscaling (dino standHeightPx ≈ 280–320,
  large cactus h ≈ 400–520, ptero wingspan ≈ 500–640, clouds ≈ 700–1000 wide, decor ≤ 300 wide).
- Encode WebP (lossy q≈88 with alpha for sprites; q≈85 for opaque backgrounds). Keep total `assets/` (excluding
  `raw/` and `previews/`) under ~8 MB.
- Consistent look across ALL assets: photorealistic, same bright hazy midday light from the upper-left, muted
  desaturated palette of the reference, pure side views (no 3/4 perspective) for dino / cacti / ptero.

## 5. Gameplay (Chrome T-Rex Runner semantics, scaled; k ≈ 2.2 world units per Chrome px)

Chrome reference constants (Chrome px, per frame @60fps): SPEED 6 → MAX_SPEED 13, ACCELERATION 0.001,
GRAVITY 0.6, INITIAL_JUMP_VELOCITY -10 (minus speed/10), MIN_JUMP_HEIGHT 30, MAX_JUMP_HEIGHT 30 (endJump sets
velocity to DROP_VELOCITY -5 once above it), SPEED_DROP_COEFFICIENT 3 (fast-fall), GAP_COEFFICIENT 0.6,
MAX_GAP_COEFFICIENT 1.5, MAX_OBSTACLE_DUPLICATION 2, CLEAR_TIME 3000 ms (no obstacles at start),
Pterodactyl min speed 8.5, altitudes (top y) [100, 75, 50] on a 150-high canvas, speed offset ±0.8, obstacle
minGap = round(width*speed + 120*0.6), maxGap = minGap*1.5. Score = distance(Chrome px) × 0.025.
Night ("invert") every 700 points, lasting ~12 s. Hi-score persisted (localStorage). GAMEOVER restart delay ~750 ms.

Scale all of these by k (tune so the realistic proportions stay fair). Requirements:
- Jump: Space / ArrowUp / W / click / tap. Variable height: releasing early after min height cuts the jump; a release
  before min height is ignored (Chrome endJump), so a very quick tap is a full jump. Only a key-up (held on the
  previous tick, released now) cuts a jump, so a buffered press released before landing still gives a full jump.
- Duck: ArrowDown / S while on ground (duck-run frames, low hitbox). In air: fast-fall.
- Obstacles: small cactus ×1–3, large cactus ×1–3 (width-dependent min speed like Chrome's multipleSpeed; members of
  one cactus group use distinct sprite variants when the variant list allows: no A-A, no A-B-A),
  pterodactyls after the speed threshold at 3 altitudes computed from the dino's actual hitboxes:
  low (must jump), mid (duck or jump), high (passes over a standing dino).
- Collision: AABB of each hitbox rect vs each obstacle rect (current frames). Must feel fair: never die to
  transparent pixels.
- Every generated obstacle sequence must be clearable by a perfect player at every speed (verified in
  tools/sim_test.js with a bot across many seeds up to max speed).
- Score HUD: 5 digits, zero-padded, "HI xxxxx yyyyy". Blink the current score 3–4 times on each 100 milestone.
- Day→night cycle every 700 points (Chrome's invert): smooth transition day → dusk → night (moon + stars) → dawn → day.
- Game over: crash pose, camera shake (respect prefers-reduced-motion), dust, "GAME OVER" + restart icon; restart
  with Space/Enter/ArrowUp/click/tap after the delay. A mid-air crash settles onto the top of the cactus it hit (or
  stays where it hit if already below that top) instead of dropping through it; ptero hits and ground crashes settle
  to the ground.
- Idle start screen: standing dino (gentle breathing bob), scenery static, hint "Press Space to play" (Japanese
  "スペースキーでスタート" / "タップでスタート" when the UI language is ja, see Language; touch devices say tap).
- Pause on tab hidden / window blur; resume on focus + input. P / Esc toggle pause (M and Shift+D do not resume;
  nor does a modifier key on its own, e.g. the Shift keydown of Shift+D).
  Clamp dt so nothing teleports.
- Audio: WebAudio synthesized (jump blip, 100-point double blip, crash thud) — created lazily on the first gesture and
  resumed from any non-running state ('suspended', WebKit's 'interrupted') on activation-triggering events (keydown,
  pointerdown, pointerup, touchend; also on becoming visible again). Sounds are scheduled only on a running clock (or
  within 400 ms of a resume request), never piled up on a stalled one. `M` toggles mute (persisted); touch screens also
  get a speaker button (≥ 44 CSS px, top left on the HUD line) on the start, pause and GAME OVER screens — never during
  a run — whose press toggles mute and is not a game press. No audio files.
- Touch: any tap starts / restarts; during a run, press-and-hold in the duck zone = duck, a duck-zone tap in the air =
  fast-fall, and a touch anywhere else jumps. The duck zone (`TOUCH_DUCK_ZONE`) is the bottom third of the LEFT half of
  the canvas, so the right thumb, and a tap near the ground on the right, always jump. Touch players see the zone as a
  faint dashed frame with a "hold to duck" label on the start screen, while paused, over the first seconds of the
  session's first run, and on GAME OVER until they have ducked by touch. Holds are tracked per pointer (a second finger
  lifting never releases the first). A key still held across a pause is held again on its auto-repeat (never a new
  press). Prevent page scroll/zoom on the canvas.
- Hi-score: never lowers the stored best (another tab's better score is picked up via the `storage` event and before
  saving).
- Accessibility: the canvas has a localized `aria-label` (en / ja); a polite live region announces start, pause and
  game over (with score and high score) — never per frame, and not for the bot / fast-forward / frozen pages.
- Language (`src/i18n.js`, `RDG.i18n`): `ja` or `en`. Negotiated from `navigator.languages` (else `[navigator.language]`):
  the first entry whose primary subtag is `ja` or `en` wins; none → `en` (`fr, ja` → ja; `en-GB, ja` → en). `?lang=ja|en`
  overrides and pins it. The window `languagechange` event re-negotiates live (unless pinned) and notifies
  `i18n.onChange` listeners; main.js then sets `ui.lang` (the renderer draws every canvas string in `ui.lang`, every
  frame). i18n.js owns the DOM strings: `<html lang>`, `document.title` (ja 「リアル恐竜ゲーム」, en "Real Dinosaur Game"),
  the meta description, elements marked `[data-i18n="key"]` (text) / `[data-i18n-attr="attr:key,…"]` (the canvas
  `aria-label`, the share UI), the live-region announcements and the share text. `t(key, {name})` fills `{name}`
  placeholders; a missing key falls back to English, then to the key. It runs in `<head>`, so the title never flashes
  English. index.html keeps English static defaults (no-JS / crawlers).
- Share (`src/share.js`, markup `#share-ui` in index.html, everything under `[data-ui]`):
  - `<button id="share-btn" type="button">` (inline SVG icon + シェア / Share), styled like the canvas restart icon (soft,
    light, frosted rounded rect, HUD dark grey; a little more opaque than the icon so a cactus behind it reads as a faint

    so at dusk too), ≥ 44 CSS px (label font 0.37 × its height, 15–28 px), `:focus-visible` ring, `position: fixed` (no

    image — below that HUD line and with no share slot to keep clear (on portrait phones lower than on screen; on
    desktop identical to the screen). A renderer without it: share.js copies the canvas itself (≤ maxW wide) and treats
    a `SecurityError` as null.

## 6. Test / debug hooks (URL params) — required for automated verification

- `?seed=N` deterministic RNG. `?bot=1` autopilot (jumps/ducks using sim knowledge; used by tests and demo).
- `?sim=MS` fast-forward: before the first render, run the fixed-step simulation for MS milliseconds of game time
  (implies game started; combine with `bot=1` to survive). `?freeze=1` stop the loop after the first render
  (for stable headless screenshots). `?state=idle|gameover` force a state. `?night=1` / `?dusk=1` force lighting.
- `?debug=1` draws hitboxes, GROUND_Y/HORIZON_Y lines, fps, speed. `?touch=1` shows the touch UI (touch hints,
  duck-zone frame, speaker button) on any device, for screenshots.
- `?lang=ja|en` pins the UI language (§5 Language). `?share=1` shows the GAME OVER share button on `?freeze` / `?bot`
  pages too (they leave it out otherwise), e.g. `?state=gameover&freeze=1&share=1`.
- When any test hook (state, sim, bot, freeze, hi) is present, the hi-score is shown but never saved to localStorage.
- `window.__rdg` exposes { sim, state, step(ms), config } for inspection (also `i18n`, `share`, and `result`: the last
  finished run's `{ score, best }` used by the share text).
- Missing / failed images never crash the game: draw simple placeholder shapes and log a warning. Images are
  requested in priority order (day sky, ground, mountains, dino; then obstacles, decor, clouds; the dusk / night skies
  and moon last). Images still pending once `LOAD_TIMEOUT_MS` (5 s) passes without any image arriving — or
  `LOAD_TIMEOUT_MAX_MS` (15 s) after loading starts, while a slow download keeps moving — get placeholders (listed in
  `renderer.A.missing` as "… (slow)") so the game starts, and are swapped in when they arrive.
